# Expert rubric - Furniture Technician (Assembly & Service), Kantholz Einrichtungswerke GmbH

Committed by the analyst BEFORE any Job-Attune engine run on this profile
(see committed_at in expert_ranking.json). The rubric is a holistic HR view
of the same 25 documents the tool will read; it deliberately includes the
kinds of judgment a keyword-based screener cannot make (depth vs presence of
experience, transferability of adjacent trades, trade-off between formal
qualification and proven practice).

## Criteria (100 points)

| # | Criterion | Max | What earns points |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Relevant assembly & installation experience | 30 | Years x domain proximity: kitchen / fitted / office furniture installs = full credit; general carpentry = high; adjacent trades or gig work = partial; none = 0 |
| B | Formal vocational qualification | 15 | Meister 15; Tischler/Schreiner Gesellenbrief 13; furniture-adjacent vocational training 10-12; facility services 8; none 0 |
| C | Tools & plan literacy | 15 | Full toolkit + assembly instructions + site measuring = 15; solid subset 10-13; drawings-only 5 |
| D | Customer-facing service evidence | 15 | Complaint handling / handover routine / ratings = 12-15; supervised or presentation-only = 5-8 |
| E | Mobility & physical | 10 | Class B licence + van routine + safe lifting = 8-10; licence only = 7 |
| F | German for Berlin customer-facing work | 10 | Native/C2 10; B2 9; B1 6; A2 2 |
| G | Application quality (letters) | 5 | Clarity, specificity, professionalism |

## Judgement calls documented up front

- **Trade-off A vs B**: proven practice can outrank formal qualification
  (Lena Hartmann vs Julian Reuter is decided on the full profile, not the
  missing certificate alone).
- **Language as enabler, not checkbox**: German is scored as a range. An
  experienced installer at A2 keeps most trade points but loses the
  customer-dialogue points that a customer-facing role in Berlin lives on
  (Colin Murphy, Marco Bianchi).
- **Transferability is discounted, not zeroed**: Hakan Yilmaz's plumbing
  decade earns real points for tools, plans, customer routine and licence -
  but near-zero for furniture assembly itself, because installing pipes is
  not installing kitchens.
- The corpus was authored by the analyst, so perfect blindness is
  impossible; mitigation is that this rubric and every sub-score below were
  written down and frozen before the tool ran, and the corpus is not
  modified after seeing tool output.
